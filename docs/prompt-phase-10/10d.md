TUJUAN:
Membuat backend Goods Receipt sebagai dokumen penerimaan barang.
Goods Receipt bisa dibuat dari Purchase Order atau dibuat langsung.
Di Phase 10, Goods Receipt belum membuat stock movement dan belum menambah stok.

RULES:

- Goods Receipt bisa dibuat dari Purchase Order.
- Goods Receipt bisa dibuat langsung.
- purchase_order_id nullable.
- purchase_order_line_id nullable di line.
- Goods Receipt tracking received_quantity.
- Goods Receipt update received_quantity di Purchase Order line jika berasal dari Purchase Order.
- Goods Receipt belum membuat stock_movement.
- Goods Receipt belum menambah stok inventory.
- Goods Receipt belum membuat inventory valuation journal.
- Goods Receipt bisa menjadi source Vendor Bill di Phase 10E.
- Partial receipt harus didukung.
- Multiple goods receipts dari satu Purchase Order harus didukung.
- Quantity receipt tidak boleh melebihi remaining quantity Purchase Order.

STATUS:

- draft
- received
- partially_billed
- billed
- cancelled
- void
